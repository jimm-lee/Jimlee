## 3. Storytelling angles

The first angle for each idea is the one its brief uses.

### #9 Coffee and Friends

- **The Confession** (used): You'd share a personal struggle with feeling stuck in routine and how it's been alleviated by grabbing coffee with friends *Hook:* “I've been stuck in a rut, meeting the same old mates for coffee every week. But then I discovered this hidden gem...”
- **The Contrarian Take**: You'd take a contrarian stance on the idea that coffee shops are a necessary evil, and instead share your go-to spot *Hook:* “I know everyone says the best coffee shops are the ones that are always crowded, but mine's the one that's quiet and worth the wait”
- **The Before-and-After Transformation**: You'd show the audience how grabbing coffee with friends completely flipped your day around *Hook:* “One of my worst days turned into one of my best when I stumbled upon this little cafe with friends”
- **The POV**: You'd put yourself in the shoes of a friend and recount a memorable coffee date *Hook:* “I was stuck in a meeting when my mate surprised me with a coffee break at this amazing new spot”

### #15 Fight Night Frenzy

- **Contrarian Take** (used): I'd argue that fight nights are the perfect antidote to a sedentary lifestyle *Hook:* “People say fight nights are just about the bloodsport, but I see it as a chance to shake off the week and live a little”
- **Before-and-After Transformation**: I'll share a dramatic shift in how I used to spend my Friday nights vs. how I do now *Hook:* “From Netflix binges to screaming at the TV with a pint in hand, I used to live for Friday nights, but then I discovered something else”
- **List**: I'll count down the top 3 reasons why I love fight nights *Hook:* “If you're anything like me, you need a night out that's more than just a drink and a screen, so here's my top 3 for why I'm hooked on fight nights”
- **POV**: I'll put the viewer in the shoes of a seasoned fight night attendee, sharing the thrill and camaraderie *Hook:* “You're the guy on the edge of your seat, screaming at the screen, and the beer in your hand is getting colder by the minute”

### #7 Sweat, Snot, and Glory

- **Confession** (used): The story is told from the perspective of a raw, honest confession, with the goal of building trust and relatability. *Hook:* “I'm not gonna lie, ice baths still freak me out, but they've become the one thing that gets me out of bed in the morning”
- **Before-and-After Transformation**: The story showcases a dramatic transformation, with the goal of inspiring and motivating the audience. *Hook:* “If you thought ice baths were just for professional athletes, think again”
- **Contrarian Take**: The story presents an unconventional viewpoint, with the goal of sparking curiosity and debate. *Hook:* “Why do we still think ice baths are only for the elite, when they're actually a lifesaver for us mere mortals”
- **Small Everyday Moment that Stands for Something Bigger**: The story highlights an ordinary moment that reveals a deeper truth or insight, with the goal of inspiring introspection and self-reflection. *Hook:* “For me, ice baths are about more than just recovery – they're about finding the courage to push past the discomfort”

### #4 Fuel for the Soul

- **Ordinary Story, Big Impact** (used): Tell a relatable story that showcases how this small everyday adventure makes a big difference in your life. *Hook:* “I'm not just fueling up for the drive, I'm fueling up for the day ahead.”
- **Fuel for the Soul: A Confession**: Share a personal confession about how your go-to breakfast spots on the road help you overcome a particular challenge or mindset. *Hook:* “I'll be the first to admit, some mornings I'm a hot mess – but a good breakfast always puts me back on track.”
- **Before-and-After Breakfast**: Show a split-screen or before-and-after style comparison of how your breakfast spots have impacted your daily routine or mindset. *Hook:* “Morning coffee and cake: from sluggish to supercharged – my go-to breakfast spots are a total game-changer.”
- **Contrarian Take: Why I Love Road Trip Breakfasts**: Take a contrarian view on why people often overlook the importance of breakfast on road trips, and make a case for why it's essential. *Hook:* “You might think road trip breakfasts are just a guilty pleasure, but I'm here to tell you – they're a necessity.”

### #5 Mountain Moments

- **Confession** (used): I share a personal struggle with feeling stuck in routine and how being in nature helped me find my way *Hook:* “I've been stuck in a rut, but a solo hike changed everything”
- **Contrarian take**: I challenge the common myth that solo hiking is only for experienced adventurers and show that it's accessible to anyone *Hook:* “Don't believe the hype - I'm a city boy who found peace on a solo mountain hike”
- **Before-and-after transformation**: I take you on a solo hike and show how the experience transformed my mind and body *Hook:* “From burnt-out to refreshed: my solo hike on the mountains”
- **POV**: I put the viewer in my shoes and take them on a solo hike, sharing my thoughts and feelings along the way *Hook:* “I'll take you on this solo hike and show you what it's really like”
