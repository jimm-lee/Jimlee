### #5 The Weight of It All

- **The Weight of It All (Confession)** (used): The creator opens up about their mental health struggles and how the weighted vest is affecting them. *Hook:* “I was struggling to breathe, and I couldn't tell anyone, but the weight of the vest was a silent killer.”
- **The Weight of It All (Before-and-After Transformation)**: The creator shows the impact of wearing the weighted vest on their mental state, from frustration to acceptance. *Hook:* “One day I woke up, and the weight wasn't just on my back - it was on my shoulders, but I was still standing.”
- **The Weight of It All (Contrarian Take)**: The creator argues that the weighted vest is actually helping them cope with stress and anxiety. *Hook:* “They say I'm crazy for doing this, but the weight is my therapy - my therapy that makes me want to scream.”
- **The Weight of It All (POV)**: The creator puts themselves in the shoes of someone struggling with mental health and shares their own experience. *Hook:* “I know what it's like to feel like you're drowning in a sea of expectations - and that's exactly what the weight feels like.”

### #2 The Struggle is Real

- **Before-and-After Transformation**: The creator shares a moment of intense struggle with the weighted vest, but contrasts it with a previous, more carefree moment. *Hook:* “I used to think running in the rain was fun, but now I'm not so sure...”
- **Confession**: The creator opens up about their own struggles with the weighted vest, making it a more relatable and honest moment. *Hook:* “I'm not gonna lie, some days it feels like I'm carrying 30kg of rocks on my back.”

### #20 The Fight Before Movember

- **Confession** (used): The creator shares a personal struggle or fear related to the fight, and how the weighted vest helped them overcome it. *Hook:* “To be honest, I've always been terrified of being punched in the face.”
- **Before-and-After Transformation**: The creator shares their experience of training with the weighted vest and how it transformed them for the fight. *Hook:* “Fast-forward to 16 days before the fight, and I'm now more ready than ever.”

- **Small Everyday Moment** (used): The story would be told through a serene and intimate atmosphere, showcasing the creator's nighttime routine as a means to relax and recharge after a day of intense hill climbing. *Hook:* “When I'm done with the hills, I'm just done. No more hills for the night.”
