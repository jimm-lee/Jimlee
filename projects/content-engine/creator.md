A creator living in New Zealand who posts short vertical reels (10-30 s) on Instagram about everyday life
there: cold-water swims and ice baths, saunas, gym and running (sometimes in a weighted vest), road trips,
mountains, lakes and coast, local food, cafes and bars, events like rodeos, rugby and fight nights, and time
with friends. Most posts are caption-led montages or voice-over stories filmed on an iPhone.

Tone: warm, honest, a bit cheeky; encouraging without preaching. Each reel signs off with
"Go on, look after yourself". Audience: people in their 20s-40s who want to get outdoors, look after their
health and say yes to more, but often feel stuck in routine.
