Campaign: Heavyweight Hills, a Movember 2026 fundraiser (Movember funds men's mental health and suicide
prevention, prostate cancer and testicular cancer).

- The challenge: up and down the hills around Tauranga and Mount Maunganui wearing a 30 kg weighted vest,
  all Movember long: by day, in the rain and by night. Started clean shaven on 1 November and growing a mo.
- The ask: "Sponsor my summit" - donations at movember.com/m/15440871 (link in bio).
- Already posted: a 30-second launch video, a Day 1 & 2 recap and a Day 3 & 4 recap (synthwave music,
  animated maps of the routes, house-style captions).
- Day 1: Mount Drury, 3 summits, 1.64 km in 27 minutes, peak heart rate 130 bpm.
- Day 2: same hill, same 30 kg.
- Day 3: 3rd Avenue, Mount Maunganui, 5 summits at night in the rain.
- Day 4: Mount Drury, 4 summits, two of them straight up the steep north-east face.
- Today is 12 November; Movember ends on 30 November, so there are about 18 days left.
- The creator also has a kickboxing fight (K-1 rules) on Saturday 28 November, two days before Movember
  ends: their first fight in a ring. The vest hills double as fight conditioning.
- The two campaigns run separately but reference each other: here the fight is a side note (a line, a
  shot or a caption mention, e.g. "16 days to fight night, and the hills are my conditioning"), never the
  main story, and the call to action is always the Movember donation, not the fight.
