payoff → linger) and the same finish: Bebas Neue captions word by word, one green word with the dark-cherry outline,
no name, closing on **"Go on, look after yourself"** over an open landscape, fade to black.

## Rules that hold for all of them

- **The first frame is the hook:** an image that's unexpected before anyone reads (jellyfish, splash, a wall of rock),
  with the first caption chunk already on it.
- **Alternate:** close/wide, still/moving, dark/light, alone/with people. Never two landscapes or two foods in a row
  in the build.
- **Cuts speed up into the payoff** (1 s → 0.5 s), then slow down: payoff 1–1.5 s, linger 2.5–3 s.
- **Captions:** one hook line, 2–3 words per chunk, word by word, one green word; nothing in the payoff; closing line on
  the linger.
- **No name, no handle.** The edit ends on the life.

## Shots that would strengthen future edits

The library is strong on landscape and big moments. 7815 (the ice tub, close) is the first true close-up, but it's
still thin on **close human detail** (the brief's "build intimacy"):
hands on the pads or the bath rim, breath in the cold, a laugh with a mate, feet hitting sand, pouring a coffee.
Two seconds of each, held steady, would give every composition above its intimate middle.
